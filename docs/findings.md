# Findings — Brightline Electronics Sales Analysis

Based on 52 orders and 81 line items across 15 customers and 14 products.

## 1. Overall Performance

- **Total Revenue:** R535,305.00
- **Total Orders:** 52
- **Average Order Value:** R10,294.33

Order values are heavily influenced by high-ticket electronics (laptops, tablets, smartphones), which pulls the average up compared to accessory-only orders.

Revenue fluctuates rather than trending in one direction, with a dip in June 2026 followed by the strongest month in the dataset (August 2026). With a full year of data, this pattern would be worth investigating for seasonality — e.g. back-to-school or promotional periods driving the August spike.

## Recommendations

- **Double down on Electronics** — it's the clear revenue driver; ensure stock and marketing prioritize this category.
- **Use Accessories as an upsell**, not a standalone revenue line — bundle with electronics purchases to lift average order value.
- **Investigate the August spike** — if it's tied to a promotion or seasonal event, plan to replicate it; if organic, understand what drove it.
- **Build a retention programme around top repeat customers** like Nomvula Dlamini, who already represent a disproportionate share of revenue.
